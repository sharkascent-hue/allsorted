How high can a garden fence be in Ireland?

The general height limits for garden fences and walls before planning permission comes into it.

Fencing

It is one of the first questions we get asked. In most cases you can put up or replace a garden fence without planning permission, as long as you stay within the standard height limits.

The general rule

  • Back and side gardens: up to 2 metres high.
  • Front gardens: up to 1.2 metres high in front of the house.

These limits come from the exempted development rules for houses. Anything higher normally needs planning permission.

When the rule may not apply

  • Protected structures and homes in architectural conservation areas.
  • Estates where the original planning permission has its own conditions on boundaries.
  • Corner sites, where a fence could block the view of drivers at a junction.

Talk to your neighbour

If the fence sits on a shared boundary, have a chat with your neighbour before work starts. Agreeing the line, the height and which side faces where avoids nearly every dispute.

This is a general guide, not legal advice. If you are unsure about your own property, check with the planning section of your local council before going ahead.
