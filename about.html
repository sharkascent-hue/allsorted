About Us

A Dublin team that fits fences and builds gardens the way we would want our own done.

Who we are

Local, hands-on and easy to deal with.

All Sorted Fencing & Landscaping is a Dublin-based company. We fit timber, composite and PVC fencing and take care of the garden around it — patios, driveways, lawns and planting.

The name says how we like to leave a job: finished, tidy and with nothing left for you to chase up.

How we work

What you can count on.

Straight answers

A clear written price, and honest advice on what is worth spending on and what is not.

Proper materials

Pressure-treated timber, concrete posts and gravel boards, and quality composite and PVC systems.

Tidy finish

Old fencing and waste removed, the garden swept, and a walk-round with you before we go.
