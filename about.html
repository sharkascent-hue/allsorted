About Us

A Dublin team that looks after fences, gardens and the outside of your home the way we would want our own done.

Who we are

Local, hands-on and easy to deal with.

All Sorted Fencing & Landscaping is a Dublin-based company. We fit fencing, build and look after gardens, lay paving, paint inside and out and power wash — so one call covers everything outside your front door.

The name says how we like to leave a job: finished, tidy and with nothing left for you to chase up.

How we work

What you can count on.

Straight answers

A clear written price, and honest advice on what is worth spending on and what is not.

Proper materials

Good timber, concrete posts, a solid base under paving and quality paint. The parts you cannot see matter most.

Tidy finish

Waste removed, the place swept, and a walk-round with you before we go.
