Questions

The things people ask us most.

Are quotes free?

Yes. We call out, measure up and give you a written quote with no obligation.

What services do you offer?

Fencing, landscaping, paving, painting, exterior cleaning and garden care. See all services.

Can you do several jobs together?

Yes — that is what we are here for. Doing the fence, paving and planting as one project usually saves time and mess.

Do you take away the old fence and waste?

We can remove and dispose of your existing fence, old paving and garden waste as part of the job — just mention it when you get in touch.

Which fence type is best for me?

Timber is the most affordable and natural looking; composite and PVC cost more up front but need almost no upkeep. We bring samples and talk you through it on the site visit.

How long does a fence take to fit?

Most garden fences are finished in one to three days, depending on length, ground conditions and the style chosen.

Do I need planning permission for a fence?

Usually not for fences up to 2 metres at the back and side or 1.2 metres at the front. See our guide to fence heights.

Do you do regular garden maintenance?

Yes. We do weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits as well as one-off tidy-ups.

Ready to get it sorted?

Free site visit and a clear written quote, anywhere in Dublin.
