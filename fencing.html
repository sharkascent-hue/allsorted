Fencing

Timber, composite and PVC fencing and gates, supplied and fitted across Dublin.

What we fit

Fences built to stand
up to Dublin weather.

Privacy, security and a boundary that looks the part. We supply and fit every style on solid concrete or timber posts.

Timber fencing

Pressure-treated panels, hit-and-miss, wave-top and lattice designs on concrete posts and gravel boards.

Composite fencing

Low-maintenance slatted boards in grey, green or wood-effect finishes. No painting, no rot.

PVC fencing & gates

Clean, bright PVC privacy fencing with lattice tops and matching side gates that wipe clean.
