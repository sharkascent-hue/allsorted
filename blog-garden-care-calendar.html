A simple garden care calendar for Irish gardens

What to do in each season to keep a garden tidy without it taking over your weekends.

Garden Care

Most gardens only need a handful of jobs done at the right time. This is the rough calendar we work to.

Spring

  • First cuts of the lawn, with the blades set high
  • Edge the lawn and weed the beds before weeds seed
  • Mulch borders to hold moisture and keep weeds down

Summer

  • Regular mowing — little and often is better than a hard cut
  • Trim fast-growing hedges, checking first for nesting birds
  • Keep on top of weeds in paving and gravel

Autumn

  • Clear fallen leaves from lawns, paths and drains
  • Main hedge cut once the nesting season is over
  • A good time to lay new turf and plant hedging

Winter

  • Prune dormant shrubs and trees where needed
  • Check fences, gates and posts after storms
  • Plan any bigger landscaping work for the year ahead

If you would rather not think about any of it, a regular visit through the growing season covers the lot.
