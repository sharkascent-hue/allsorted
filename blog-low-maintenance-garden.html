Five ways to make a garden lower maintenance

Design choices that cut down the weekly work without turning the garden into a car park.

Landscaping

A low-maintenance garden is not one with nothing in it. It is one where the jobs that eat your time have been designed out.

1. Shrink the awkward bits of lawn

Narrow strips and tight corners are slow to mow. Turning them into gravel or planting leaves a simpler lawn shape that takes minutes to cut.

2. Edge everything

A firm edge between lawn, beds and gravel stops grass creeping and keeps stone where it belongs. It is the single biggest saver on tidying time.

3. Use membrane and mulch

Weed membrane under gravel and a thick mulch on beds stop most weeds before they start.

4. Plant in groups

A few hardy shrubs and grasses planted in blocks cover the ground, look deliberate and need far less attention than lots of small, different plants.

5. Choose the boundary wisely

A composite or PVC fence needs a wash now and then. A timber fence needs treating, and a hedge needs cutting. Pick the one that matches the time you actually have.
