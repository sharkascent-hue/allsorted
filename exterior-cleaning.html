Exterior Cleaning

Looks new again,
without replacing it.

Power washing for driveways, patios, roofs, walls and decking.

Driveways

Oil marks, tyre tracks, moss and weeds lifted from tarmac, concrete and block paving.

Patios & paths

Green, slippery slabs brought back to their original colour and made safe to walk on.

Walls & render

House and garden walls cleaned at the right pressure so the surface is not damaged.

Decking & fences

Timber cleaned gently and left ready for staining or treatment.

Re-sanding

Block paving joints re-sanded after cleaning so the blocks stay locked in place.

Roof cleaning

Moss and dirt cleared from roof tiles and ridge lines.

Careful work

The right pressure for each surface.

  • Lower pressure on render, pointing and timber
  • Drains and gullies kept clear of debris
  • Doors, windows and cars rinsed down after
  • Advice on sealing or treating once it is clean
