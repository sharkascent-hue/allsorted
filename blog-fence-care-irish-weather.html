How to look after a timber fence in Irish weather

A few small jobs each year that add years to the life of a timber fence.

Fencing

Rain and wind are what wear a timber fence out. A little attention once or twice a year makes a real difference to how long it lasts.

Keep timber off the soil

Timber that sits in damp earth rots first. If soil, bark or leaves have built up against the bottom of your panels, rake them back. Concrete gravel boards under the panels solve this for good.

Treat it in dry weather

Give the fence a coat of a good fence treatment or stain every two to three years. Pick a dry spell, brush off any green growth first, and pay extra attention to the tops and end grain where water soaks in.

Cut back plants

Ivy and heavy climbers hold moisture against the timber and add weight in the wind. Keep hedges and shrubs trimmed a little away from the panels so air can move.

Check after storms

After a big wind, walk the fence. Look for posts that have started to lean, loose panels and missing caps. A loose panel fixed this week is far cheaper than a run of fence that comes down in the next storm.
