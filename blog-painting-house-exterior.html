When is the best time to paint the outside of a house in Ireland?

Picking the right weather window, and the preparation that makes exterior paint last.

Painting

Exterior paint fails early for two reasons: it went on in the wrong weather, or it went on over a surface that was not ready. Both are avoidable.

Pick the right window

Late spring to early autumn is the usual season. You are looking for a dry spell with mild temperatures, and walls that have had time to dry out after rain. Paint applied to damp masonry or just before a shower will not bond properly.

Avoid the extremes

  • Too cold or damp: paint cures slowly and can wash off or bloom.
  • Hot, direct sun: paint dries too fast and can blister or show lap marks.
  • Late evening: dew can settle on paint before it has dried.

Preparation is most of the job

Walls should be washed down to remove dirt, moss and algae, then left to dry. Flaking paint is scraped back, cracks are filled, and chalky or bare patches get a stabilising primer. Only then do two full coats of masonry paint go on.

How long should it last?

That depends on the paint, the wall and how exposed the house is, but good preparation and a quality masonry paint will always outlast a quick coat over a dirty wall.
